Research & Project Experience

Research Internship

  • Research Intern · The Chinese University of Hong Kong
    Apr 2026 – Aug 2026 · Led two studies as first author

    CVM: Auditing LLM Essay Graders

    Agreement with human scores can hide two failures: a grader may ignore content loss or reward irrelevant text. I designed an audit that tests both responses and introduced the Construct Validity Margin (CVM) to compare score drops and gains. The audit covered eight graders. Among grader and prompt combinations with at least moderate agreement with human scores, gains from irrelevant text matched or exceeded losses from content removal in 18/27 on ASAP and 16/23 on PERSUADE 2.0.

    I also developed a CVM-based contrastive objective for fine tuning Qwen2.5-7B, reducing score inflation from keyword stuffing not seen during training.

    EJRD: Interpreting Changes in Job Requirements

    More mentions of a job requirement do not necessarily mean broader employer demand. I built the Emerging Job Requirement Dataset (EJRD) from Hong Kong job postings and developed the Scope-Aware Validity Audit (SAVA). The framework evaluates mention growth and employer counts using human judgments of novelty and questionnaire records. Employer counts better identified requirements associated with questionnaire use. A manual audit of 200 cases also showed that a questionnaire's presence did not necessarily mean it assessed the requirement.

Projects & Competitions

  • Opinion as Prior: Supervised VA Prediction for DimASQP
    Dec 2025 – May 2026 · First author · ICONIP 2026

    Different opinions about the same aspect can express different emotions. I introduced direct supervision of scores predicted from opinion text, followed by refinement using the corresponding aspect. On samples correctly extracted by all compared models, mean Euclidean error fell by 3.4% on Restaurant and 7.5% on Laptop relative to the baseline.

  • CyberAI Cup 2026 · Ranked 8th Worldwide
    Jul 2026 – Aug 2026 · Team lead, SZTU-AGI · Responsible for packaging image detection and encrypted traffic classification
    • Packaging image difference detection: To distinguish small packaging changes from misalignment, lighting, and noise, I combined image registration, difference maps, filtering, and a U-Net ensemble. On a harder internal validation split, the three-model ensemble reduced false positives from 574 for a single model to 30.
    • Encrypted traffic classification: To identify call applications without access to packet contents, I designed 51 features from the first five packets and combined LightGBM, XGBoost, and TabICL v2. In internal flow-level cross-validation, the features improved TabICL v2's macro F1 from 0.788 to approximately 0.823.
